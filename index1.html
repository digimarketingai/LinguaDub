Subtitling Comedy in the Age of AI: MT Problems & Post-Editing SkillsAI 時代的喜劇字幕翻譯：機器翻譯問題與譯後編輯技巧

This module uses a short Chinese stand-up clip to show what machine translation (MT) and large language models (LLMs) get wrong when they translate humour. It also teaches the professional post-editing skills needed to fix those mistakes for subtitles and AI dubbing.

本單元以一段中文脫口秀片段為例，說明機器翻譯（MT）與大型語言模型（LLM）翻譯幽默時常出的錯，並教授修正這些錯誤所需的專業譯後編輯技巧，涵蓋字幕與 AI 配音。

Humour Translation · 幽默翻譯

Anatomy of the 面膜 pun「面膜」雙關解析

The joke splits a compound word in two. Each half means something ordinary on its own, but together they mean "face mask", the one product a self-described plain-looking man shouldn't sell. MT can't see this, because it translates the meaning of the compound and loses the split.

這個笑話把一個複合詞拆成兩半：每一半單獨看都很普通，合起來卻是「面膜」，正是一個自認長相平凡的人不該賣的產品。機器翻譯看不出這點，因為它翻的是整個複合詞的意思，拆字的巧思因此消失。

💡 Simplified vs Traditional script💡 簡繁差異的教學重點

In Simplified Chinese, 面 means both "face" and "noodles", so the pun works in both speech and writing. In Traditional Chinese, "noodles" is written 麵, so the pun only works by sound. If you subtitle this clip in Traditional Chinese, writing 賣麵 makes the split visible, while writing 賣面 is a typo for most Taiwanese and Hong Kong readers. Either way, the written pun is weaker, which is an extra problem for anyone localizing within Chinese.

簡體的「面」同時指臉與麵條，因此雙關在口語和文字上都成立；繁體中，麵條寫作「麵」，雙關只剩讀音相同。若製作繁中字幕，寫「賣麵」能讓拆字變得清楚，寫「賣面」對臺港讀者而言則像錯字；無論哪種寫法，文字層面的雙關都會減弱，這是中文內部在地化時多出的難題。

AI Translation Problems · AI 翻譯問題

What goes wrong when AI translates comedy?AI 翻譯喜劇時會出什麼錯？

Recent research agrees on a pattern. LLMs often do better than conventional neural MT on sitcom subtitles (Chen & Hu, 2026). They still struggle with puns (CLEF JOKER), with context (Vincent et al., 2024), and with errors carried over from speech recognition into subtitles (Karakanta et al., 2022). Human post-editing is still needed.

近期研究的結論一致：LLM 翻譯情境喜劇字幕常優於傳統神經機器翻譯（Chen & Hu, 2026），但在雙關（CLEF JOKER）、語境（Vincent 等，2024），以及語音辨識錯誤延伸到字幕（Karakanta 等，2022）等方面仍有困難，因此仍須人工譯後編輯。

Post-Editing Skills · 譯後編輯技巧

Post-editing: tactics and skills譯後編輯：策略與技巧

Light post-editing (ISO 18587)輕度譯後編輯

Goal: understandable and accurate. Fix meaning errors only and accept stiff style. Fits internal use or quick gisting.

目標：可理解、正確。只修正意義錯誤，可接受生硬文風。適用於內部用途或快速了解大意。

When I showed up on the livestream with face masks, the viewers were excited and posted many comments, but no comment discussed the product; all were question marks.

Full post-editing (ISO 18587)完整譯後編輯

Goal: reads like a professional human translation. Covers accuracy, style, register, segmentation, timing and humour. Required for broadcast and streaming.

目標：讀起來像專業人工翻譯，涵蓋正確性、文風、語域、斷句、時間軸與幽默。播出與串流平台必須採用。

When I showed up on the livestream
holding face masks, / the viewers got really excited.
The chat was going crazy. / But not a single comment
was about the product. / It was all question marks.

Workflow · 工作流程

AI + human workflow for subtitling and dubbingAI＋人工字幕與配音流程

🛠️ LinguaDub Studio: AI dubbing pipeline🛠️ LinguaDub Studio：AI 配音流程

LinguaDub is an open-source tool for AI video dubbing. It offers voice cloning, script editing, line timing control, and translations that fit each line's time slot. According to its README, the pipeline is Whisper (transcribe) → MyMemory / NLLB / Google (translate) → XTTS v2 (voice clone).

LinguaDub 是開源 AI 影片配音工具，提供聲音複製、腳本編輯、逐句時間控制，以及配合每句時段長度的翻譯。依其 README，流程為 Whisper（轉錄）→ MyMemory／NLLB／Google（翻譯）→ XTTS v2（聲音複製）。

Check A

ASR post-editing語音辨識校對

  • Correct names (志勝 / 志胜) and homophones校正人名（志勝／志胜）與同音字
  • Check sentence boundaries, because Whisper sometimes merges lines檢查斷句，Whisper 有時會把句子合併
  • Watch for invented text in silences or during laughter留意靜音或笑聲處被編造的文字
Check B

Script post-editing腳本譯後編輯

  • Recreate the pun so it works when spoken, which is different from subtitles重寫雙關，使其念出來也成立（與字幕不同）
  • Fit the line's time slot by shortening or expanding the text刪減或擴充文字，使其符合每句時段
  • Write for the ear, with contractions and spoken rhythm為耳朵而寫：使用縮寫與口語節奏
Check C

Audio QA音訊品管

  • Stress the punchline word ("face masks?")重音落在笑點詞上（"face masks?"）
  • Keep the comic pause before the punchline保留笑點前的喜劇停頓
  • Check for unnatural speed-ups caused by squeezing in too much text檢查文字過長而被迫加速的不自然語速

The dubbing trade-off triangle配音限制三角

What research tells us about AI dubbing研究對 AI 配音的啟示

Brannon et al. (2023) studied 319.57 hours of professional dubbing. Human dubbers cared more about vocal naturalness and translation quality than about matching character length or lip-sync. They rarely changed speaking speed or gave up translation quality to hit timing. The lesson for LinguaDub users: don't cram text into a slot. Rewrite the line instead.

Brannon 等（2023）分析 319.57 小時的專業配音，發現配音員重視語音自然度與翻譯品質，勝過字數對應與對嘴；他們很少改變語速，也很少為了配合時長犧牲翻譯品質。給 LinguaDub 使用者的啟示：不要硬把文字塞進時段，應該改寫句子。

Practice · 練習

Student tasks, rubric, checklist and quiz學生任務、評分標準、檢核表與測驗

Task 1 · 30 min

MT error annotation機翻錯誤標註

Run segment A through two engines (e.g. Google Translate and an LLM). Annotate each output with MQM categories and severity (minor / major / critical).

將 A 段分別交給兩個引擎（如 Google 翻譯與一個 LLM），以 MQM 類別與嚴重度（輕微／嚴重／重大）標註。

Task 2 · 30 min

Three pun strategies三種雙關策略

Write three versions of the 面膜 punchline: pun→pun, pun→non-pun humour, and pun→zero. Each must be 42 characters or fewer per line. Explain which one you'd submit and why.

為「面膜」笑點寫三個版本：雙關→雙關、雙關→非雙關幽默、雙關→省略，每行不超過 42 字；說明你會交哪一版及理由。

Task 3 · 45 min

Spotting & CPS上時間軸與 CPS

Time segment B against the real audio in Subtitle Edit or Aegisub. Report the CPS for each event and justify any line you cut.

在 Subtitle Edit 或 Aegisub 中依實際音訊為 B 段上時間軸，回報每個字幕事件的 CPS，並說明刪減理由。

Task 4 · 90 min

LinguaDub dubbing labLinguaDub 配音實作

Dub a 30-second clip using your own voice. Post-edit at checkpoints A, B and C. Submit the before/after scripts and a 300-word reflection on what the AI got wrong.

用自己的聲音為 30 秒片段配音，在檢查點 A、B、C 進行譯後編輯。繳交修改前後腳本與 300 字反思，說明 AI 哪裡出錯。
